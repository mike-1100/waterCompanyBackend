## Features:
- Broadcast new water additions to everyone on the blockchain.
- Transparency: consumers can verify water quality.
- Immutability: Once water quality results are recorded, they can’t be altered.
- Traceability: Each batch of water can be tracked from treatment, distribution etc.
- Public trust: Regulators and customers can independently verify records.
